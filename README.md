# Angband Master

![Angband Master's title screen](docs/title.png)

**Angband, played the Dungeon Master way.** The real Angband 4.2 engine runs
underneath, with all its monsters, items, magic and decades of balance
unchanged, while a new front end draws its dungeon in first person, in 3D:
painted monsters and items, your own weapon and hands as you fight and
cast, torchlight, weather in the town, sounds made for the game, and an
overhead map always in view.

In development. The game and its source will be published here.
